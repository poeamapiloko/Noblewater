Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
How fast is delivery?
Same-day or within 2 days for local Maseru deliveries. Orders outside Maseru are confirmed individually on WhatsApp.
Is delivery really free?
Yes, on orders of 5+ cases within Maseru. Smaller orders or deliveries outside Maseru may carry a delivery fee — we'll confirm this with you before dispatch.
How do I pay?
You can order via WhatsApp directly, or pay by M-Pesa, EcoCash, or bank transfer at checkout — we'll confirm your order once payment is received.
What if a bottle arrives damaged or leaking?
Message us on WhatsApp with a photo and your order reference — we'll replace it on your next delivery, free of charge.
Can I get a discount for regular orders?
Yes — set up a monthly standing order and save 5–10% depending on volume, plus priority delivery. Message us on WhatsApp to set it up.
Can I get custom branded bottles for an event?
Yes — minimum order is 72 bottles (3 cases). See the Custom Branding section above or WhatsApp us for a quote.
